01 — WHY WE MADE IT

Everyday academic tasks.
Easier on your phone.

Check your classroom before a lecture. Look up a grade when results arrive. Browse course details before registration. Small, everyday tasks deserve a more convenient home.

SCNU Academic (华师教务) is an unofficial Android companion for students at South China Normal University (SCNU). It brings timetables, grades, exam schedules, announcements, and course selection into a clearer, mobile-friendly interface.

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3, it focuses on the academic tasks you actually need on campus.

A convenient companion. Always confirm important details with the university's official academic system.
02 — TIMETABLE

What's next?
One glance is enough.

See your courses by week, with the current week selected automatically. Switch between weeks and open a class to check its teacher, location, periods, campus, credits, and teaching weeks.

  • Make it fit your term. Adjust the semester start date, total weeks, class times, and semester detection settings.
  • A timetable in your colors. Switch between multiple built-in course color palettes to make your weekly schedule feel more personal.
  • Take a copy with you. Export the current semester's timetable as a PDF and check the university calendar in the app.

03 — GRADES & EXAMS

More than a final score.
See the details.

Browse past grades by semester. Open a result to see coursework marks, final-exam marks, the overall grade, and the weight of each component.

Your results, broken down

View published results and second-category course grades, and export your grades as a spreadsheet. When you need official documents, apply for electronic transcripts and certificates, then preview and download the PDFs.

Know your exam arrangements

Check exam information for the current or a past semester, including time, room, seat number, and campus. Export the schedule as a spreadsheet for reference.

04 — COURSES & PROGRESS

Choose this term's courses.
See the bigger picture.

During registration, browse available courses by category, check details and capacity, submit a selection request, and confirm it in your selected-course list.

  • Save courses for later. Copy course information into your favorites. The same copy-and-paste format lets you share your saved courses across devices.
  • Explore your degree plan. View an academic overview, credit progress, and your curriculum. Expand each module and load its course list when needed.
  • Passed, in progress, or still to take. Check course status, grades, grade points, and credits, including failed and substituted courses.
  • Check filtered-out selections. Look up courses removed during selection, including the class, teacher, meeting times, and location.
05 — CAMPUS ESSENTIALS

Beyond your timetable.
The details of campus life.

Read academic announcements, shared documents, and internal messages in one place. Messages are organized as unread or read, and some notices and documents include downloadable attachments.

Everyday tasks, together

Check the calendar, manage your profile, and complete course evaluations. Add comments, save a draft, submit your evaluation, and review submissions.

An AI assistant when needed

Multiple conversations, streaming replies, Markdown, and mathematical notation are supported. The assistant is separate from academic workflows and is shown only with developer authorization.

06 — BEFORE YOU START

A little more convenience.
A few important notes.

This is an unofficial tool. It does not act as an official university service. Use it for learning and convenience only, with legitimate access to the university's academic system, and follow university rules.

Timetables, grades, and exam information come from the university's academic system. Cached data may be out of date; refresh or sign in again for the latest information. Always confirm exams, course registration, and other important matters through official university sources.

The app includes eligibility verification and device-identifier-related functionality. Features can vary by version or authorization status. Read the repository documentation before use and sign in only on trusted devices.

08 — QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

A few things you might ask.

Is this an official university app?

No. This is an unofficial academic companion for SCNU students, separate from any official university client. Refer to the official academic system and university announcements for all important matters.

Can I install it on an iPhone or computer?

Currently, only an Android APK is available, requiring Android 7.0 or later. There is no iOS or desktop version. This website introduces the app and links to its downloads; it cannot retrieve your academic records.

How do I install the downloaded app?

Download the release's .apk file from GitHub Releases and open it on your Android phone. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps. Check that the installer comes from the AlgoZZi/ScnuJwxt-APK repository.

Why are some features missing?

Availability depends on the app version, the university's service windows, and authorization status. The AI assistant requires developer authorization, and course selection is available only during the university's registration periods.

What if my data is out of date or something goes wrong?

Try refreshing or signing in again, then check your account and network connection. If the issue continues, send feedback through the app's Profile → Feedback entry (我的 → 反馈).

Is the app's source code on GitHub?

The repository currently provides project documentation and APK releases, not the app's source code. Download the installer from Releases or read the README for full feature and usage information.
